The Archive

A letter with no address.

Four volumes fill a narrow shelf. A brass bookmark rests in Volume IV, beside a letter with a curious seal.

IIIIIIIV
THE MARKED VOLUME
Open the marked volume+

The bookmark points to Volume IV. Written inside: “Keep the number of the book that kept my letter.”

Reveal the deduction

The third digit is 4. Volume IV is volume four. The journal puts the volume third.
