The story so far

The workshop is silent.
The clues are not.

At nine o’clock, every clock in Elias Wren’s workshop stopped. By morning, the clockmaker had vanished. His tools were in place. His tea was still on the desk. Only a brass vault remained locked.

Inside it lies the answer. Follow the trail through his Study, the Clock Tower, and the Archive. Collect four digits, place them in order, and discover what Wren left behind.

There is no countdown. Take your time. Every puzzle has a hint.
