# Kanban handoff board

Import or copy kanban.csv into the team’s actual board. Columns: Backlog, To Do, In Progress, Review, Done. Suggested WIP limit: one active implementation card per member, and no more than three cards in In Progress. Review cards on mobile and with a keyboard before moving them to Done.

The CSV describes this package’s current handoff status. It does not assert a historical board or past standups. Add genuine screenshots from the existing team board, with dates and card movement explanations. During a 10–15 minute standup, discuss what can finish, whether WIP is within the limit, and what is blocked. Record attendance, hours, blockers, decisions, and next actions in standup-log.csv.

## Kanban benefits to explain

Visual status makes unfinished work visible. WIP limits encourage finishing existing cards before starting more. The Review column gives responsive and accessibility checks a place in the flow. Blocker owners and next actions make follow-up concrete. Use real examples from the team’s actual board in the recording.
