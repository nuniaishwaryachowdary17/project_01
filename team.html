Under the surface

Small tools.
Deliberate choices.

HTML holds the state.

Radio buttons choose rooms and digits. Checkboxes filter evidence. Native disclosure elements reveal clues and hints. CSS responds to each state.

One visual language.

Warm brass, deep green, and parchment text connect every screen. Shared variables control color, spacing, and motion. Every page includes keyboard focus styles and reduced-motion support.

Help stays inside the story.

The Apprentice simulates an intent-based assistant. Next Step cards simulate an explainable recommendation system. Both use authored rules and content without an AI service.
